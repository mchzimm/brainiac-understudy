Brainiac Understudy

updated 2026-11-07 02:29 PST

The understudy is a small helper that answers questions about stories while the real brainiac is still growing up. You read it a story, it keeps some sentences, and then you can ask it things. It is only a stand-in, like a substitute teacher, and it is NOT brainiac: every answer starts with "[understudy, not brainiac]". When brainiac learns to answer well, the understudy goes away.
